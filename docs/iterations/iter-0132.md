## 2. The P1 — the commit appendix inherited the first slice's net

In `TestConsecutiveStatementsCannotAccumulate`, the commit-side transaction reused `cctx`
from the 250ms rollback slice. The first slice is entitled to consume its WHOLE contract, so
on a loaded runner the second transaction could start life with near-zero or already-expired
client time and false-fail with nothing wrong — and it was not the production envelope the
test claims to exercise. tx2 now mints its own `deadline2` and derives its own net from it,
exactly the production shape; the rollback accumulation half is unchanged.
