# iter-0134 — FR-021: the proofs prove and the observability obeys its own spec

## 1. Summary

The final iter-0133 verdict accepted every runtime fix and rejected the changeset on its
evidence and its §21/operations surface. This changeset closes those: the archive-race proof
now blocks AFTER the span read and is judged at the FACT; readiness has one answer; the §21
metrics are event counters where the spec means counters, sampled gauges only where a bounded
sample exists; partition adoption is fenced; the docs say exactly what was run.

## 3. What changed

**U1.** Spans are read per bucket immediately before that bucket's fact upsert, so the
regression holds the FACT ROW of a post-cancel bucket hostage: the batch reads the OLD span
for exactly the bucket whose exclusion the archive removes, then parks at its upsert
(pg_stat_activity-observed). The archive lands mid-wait; the queue is driven to quiescence;
the discriminating assertion is the FACT — at quiescence the post-cancel bucket carries zero
exclusion. The no-bump mutant now dies at "bucket F carries 60000000us of exclusion computed
under the pre-archive span": the stale fact itself, not a range state.

**U2.** `WritePrometheus` computes `cerbix_ready` from the SAME composite as `Ready()` — a
wedged scheduler can no longer fail its probe while telling Prometheus 1. The wedge rule is
`serviceWedgeReason`, deliberately narrow: a repair range terminally parked in `error`.
Watermark LAG is explicitly NOT a wedge — a service adopting ninety days of history reports
an enormous, shrinking lag while every slice advances, and one absolute sample cannot tell
that healthy backlog from a stuck materializer; until a progress-across-samples tracker
exists, lag stays a gauge with an alert suggestion. `TestOldBackfillLagIsNotWedged` is the
negative regression; the readiness-consistency assertion joined the wedged test.

**U3.** *(Metric names/semantics partly superseded in the round-2/2 fix pass — see §7; the authoritative surface is the runbook table.)* The §21 list as of round 1:
- COUNTERS at their event sites (store-side `ServiceEventsSink`, wired from the CLI):
  `cerbix_service_epoch_fanout_total` (both epoch writers),
  `cerbix_service_repair_outcomes_total{complete|failed|unrecomputable|superseded|released}`
  (the four lifecycle writers, superseded distinguished from a plain release),
  `cerbix_service_unrecomputable_ranges_total` (the §21 rejection counter),
  `cerbix_service_late_arrivals_total` + overflow (the late-arrival record, one event per
  call, overflow derived from the running arrival count).
- GAUGES only where a bounded sample exists: `cerbix_service_repair_ranges{state}` over the
  ACTIVE states (pending/running/error — the set the queue drains), and the zero-clamped
  watermark lag. Terminal states, late sums and epoch populations are never rescanned.
- The sampler runs OFF the dispatch loop, in its own goroutine per leadership (dies with the
  leadership context; step-down clears the gauges), so no observability query can hold
  dispatch against §10.10's 250ms cadence.

**U4.** *(Superseded in the round-2/2 fix pass and again by iter-0135 — kept for the round-1
record.)* The round-1 shape moved the month under one parent ACCESS EXCLUSIVE; the fix pass
batched the move; iter-0135 replaced both with the copy-authoritative design after the final
verdict showed the batched DELETE hid sealed facts mid-adoption and stranded them on a crash.
The current semantics live in iter-0135 §3.

**U5.** Docs reconciled below.

## 7. Round 1/2 disposition — the fix pass

Round 1/2 accepted U1's late barrier and the single readiness formula, and rejected on four
P1s and two P2s. All closed:

- **Sampler lifecycle (fail-closed, caller-owned).** The loop declares UNKNOWN (wedged, not
  ready) before its first sample and re-enters "unavailable" on any sample failure — a
  terminal error range persisted before this leadership cannot hide behind an empty registry,
  and a query failure cannot preserve a stale healthy verdict. The lifecycle is cancel+JOIN
  before `lead` returns, so the step-down clear can never race a completing sample. Three
  deterministic regressions: fail-closed-until-success, failure-marks-unavailable,
  join-precedes-clear (an in-flight sample provably cannot publish after the clear).
- **The snapshot is bounded and pinned.** Migration 00074 adds the terminal-error partial
  index and the watermark expression index; every state count is an index-backed probe
  SATURATING at 1000, the lag is one index probe of `min(COALESCE(sealed_through,
  era_start))`, and the event totals read a three-row persisted aggregate.
  `TestReliabilityStatsPlansAreIndexBounded` pins the plans with seqscan disabled.
- **The §21 counters count their contract.** `cerbix_service_unrecomputable_rejections_total`
  records the MUTATION refusal at the `ErrUnrecomputableRange` boundaries (preview + both
  confirm floors), commit-independently — distinct from a range parking on evidence loss.
  Lifecycle outcomes carry `{outcome, reason}`: the range reason is what tells a repair from
  a recompute. Epoch fan-out and late arrivals moved into `service_metric_events`, bumped IN
  the owning transaction — a rollback takes the delta with it — and exported by the sampler
  as monotonic counters; the rollback-negative regression is committed, and the late-arrival
  overflow derives from the running arrival count in the same statement.
- **Adoption without an outage.** The month's data moves in bounded 500-row batches with NO
  parent lock; only the cutover (final sweep + ATTACH) takes ACCESS EXCLUSIVE, under
  `statement_timeout 5s` / `lock_timeout 2s`; the whole pass runs in its own per-leadership
  maintenance goroutine, out of the dispatch loop. The reworked fence test holds a ROW in
  the DEFAULT child, parks the batch move on it (observed), and proves a mid-move parent
  insert completes in under two seconds — the outage is structurally gone — then attaches
  and finds every row in the month partition.
- **Docs.** The wedged HELP line, the runbook's saturation/attribution/adoption wording, and
  the traceability row's scope were corrected; §6's dev-test row reads the executed result.
