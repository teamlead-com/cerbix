## 3. What changed

**P1-1.** `date_trunc('month', timestamptz)` truncates in the SESSION zone: under Asia/Baku,
December's stranded facts were named 202511 — the wrong month adopted, the real one
unreachable. Recovery now truncates the UTC PROJECTION (`min(bucket_start) AT TIME ZONE
'UTC'`), and migration 00064's seed computes its months from `now() AT TIME ZONE 'UTC'` and
emits bounds with an explicit `+00` offset — session-proof on a fresh non-UTC database.
`TestPastRecoveryIsSessionTimezoneProof` runs the whole rollover on a Baku-session store
(the reviewer's product repro shape) and asserts the UTC month attached AND the session-zone
month absent; `TestMigrationSeedExpressionsAreUTC` pins the seed's expressions at the
discriminating boundary instant and greps the shipped file. The session-local mutant dies on
"the UTC month was not the one recovered". Note: the seed fix covers fresh databases; no
non-UTC installation exists (beta, dev/CI are UTC), stated rather than implied away.

**P1-2.** The cutover ran LOCK, sweep and ATTACH under one `SET statement_timeout=5s` —
which RESTARTS per statement: the "≤5s" parent-lock claim was unprovable, and this is the
same re-minting lesson as iter-0130's lifecycle writes. The fence now runs through
`deadlineTx` with ONE absolute `adoptionFenceBudget` (5s) from Begin through COMMIT —
remainder-derived server bounds per statement, the client net one tolerance behind. The
aggregate-budget mechanism proof is `TestConsecutiveStatementsCannotAccumulate` (two
near-deadline statements under one absolute deadline, the second cut by the remainder);
`TestAdoptionCutoverUsesTheDeadlineMechanism` pins the fence to it structurally and rejects
any per-statement SET LOCAL timeout (the reverted-fence mutant fails both assertions); the
bounded-abort behavioral test stands. The runbook states the transaction-wide bound.

**P1-3.** The copy resumes from DURABLE progress: the staging's own max `(bucket_start,
service_id)` key is the resume point, so an interrupted pass skips its copied prefix
entirely — correctness below the resume point belongs to the authoritative fence, by
construction. The unconditional prefix re-upsert (a livelock for any month larger than one
maintenance pass) is gone; `TestAdoptionResumesPastTheCopiedPrefix` proves the prefix's xmin
untouched across a resume, and the zero-cursor mutant dies on "the copied prefix was
REWRITTEN". The orphan anti-join sweeps run only until both FKs exist VALIDATED (checked in
pg_constraint) — once they do, new orphans are impossible and the whole-staging rescans were
pure retry cost. `recoverPastFactMonth` now runs BEFORE the current..ahead loop, so a hot
current month cannot starve the one-unit-per-cadence catch-up.

**P2.** The standalone probe joins the parent's own namespace by OID (the "schema-qualified"
claim is now true of EVERY probe); traceability counts the mutant roster exactly (nineteen
named, twenty-two with this round's three); status AC-0133-2 rewritten to the persisted-
aggregate/saturating-sample semantics; and the [142] stuck signal exists as metrics:
`cerbix_service_fact_maintenance_failing` + `_last_success_timestamp_seconds` — one boolean
and one timestamp, low-cardinality by construction, set by the maintenance loop, cleared on
step-down, with the runbook alert `failing == 1 AND last-success older than 30m`.
`TestFactMaintenanceStuckSignal` pins that a failure never advances last-success.

## 7. Round 1/2 disposition — the fix pass

Round 1/2 accepted the UTC direction (independently confirmed on a fresh Baku-session
migration) and rejected on two P1 contracts and four P2s. All closed:

- **P1 (oversize livelock).** Hole-free incremental shrinking of the fenced workload does
  not exist under native partitioning — any row leaving DEFAULT before ATTACH vanishes from
  parent reads, which is the P0 this design replaced. So the supported bound is DECLARED and
  GATED: a month whose remaining DEFAULT rows exceed `adoptionFenceMaxRows` (100k) is refused
  BEFORE the parent lock — no doomed all-row DELETE ever repeats — and the runbook now splits
  recovery precisely: hot-under-bound (quiesce + cadences, monotonic resume) versus oversize
  (a maintenance-window psql cutover, exact statements given). The committed regression
  drives BOTH cadences of the gate (no fence attempt, facts fully visible) and then executes
  the operator cutover; the gate-removal mutant dies on "an
  oversize month cut through the fence gate".
  > **Corrected by the FINAL review [152] and iter-0137:** "verbatim" overstated the
  > evidence — the regression expanded private Go constants, not the runbook text, and the
  > runbook text itself carried placeholders an operator could not run. Both are superseded:
  > the psql procedure is deleted, the recovery artifact is the shipped
  > `cerbix adopt-fact-month` command (spec §10.11, D-0161), the gate is re-checked UNDER the
  > parent lock (the unlocked count alone was a TOCTOU), and the artifact itself is tested
  > end-to-end. See iter-0137.
- **P1 (caller-capped fence).** The fence deadline is `min(now+budget, caller deadline −
  tolerance)` with an exhausted tail refused BEFORE Begin (claimRepairRangeBounded is the
  precedent) — the server is never told it may run for seconds while the client net cancels
  in milliseconds. `TestFenceRefusesAnExhaustedCallerTail` hands the fence a spent caller and
  asserts the budget refusal, never a net kill; the uncapped mutant dies on exactly that.
- **P2 (FK by definition, fail closed).** The sweep-skip verifies contype, referenced
  relation, delete action and deferrability — never names — and a constraint SQUATTING on a
  reserved name with the wrong definition fails the adoption closed instead of skipping the
  sweep and swallowing the real key as a duplicate. Negative regression + name-count mutant.
- **P2 (alert semantics).** Tracking start floors last-success, so the age-based alert
  cannot fire on a first-cadence blip; the initial-failure case is the new first assertion
  of the metric test, and the floor-removal mutant dies there.
- **P2 (evidence).** The status AC-0133-2 rewrite is now real (the previous edit was a
  silent-noop regex — my own docs-drift pattern, caught and asserted this time). The UTC
  seed's durable evidence is the REAL migration: `TestFreshMigrationUnderNonUTCSessionSeeds
  UTCBounds` creates a fresh database, migrates 00001→latest under a Baku session, and
  inspects the live `pg_get_expr` bounds — the session-local seed mutant dies showing the
  actual `+04` bounds. Traceability counts are exact again (36 regressions, twenty-seven
  named mutants).
