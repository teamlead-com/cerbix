## 1. The answer to the question

No, this is not Grafana's territory, and the argument is not "it is only one panel". The line is
drawn explicitly and permanently in the specification (§3): no query builder, no time-range picker
on these panels, no drag-zoom, no panel composition, no second metric or overlaid series. What
justified the work is that the panel was **less defensible than the table beside it** — it dropped
an enumerable class of real failures and had no time axis at all.

## 2. What reading the code found, beyond the two complaints

Verified against `7cfb2ec`; the numbers are measured, not estimated.

- **The timeline's x axis was bucket-count space.** `reliabilityStepRollupTx` emits a point only for
  steps that HAVE buckets, and the strip laid those out consecutively by count, so a 30-day window
  over a service with two and a half days of facts filled its whole width while the card's own KPI
  directly above it read `storage 2873 of 43200 buckets`.
- **The gaps between the slabs were decoration.** Padding was 8% of each tick's own width, a
  hairline across ninety ticks and a canyon across three. Confirmed by pixel-scanning the owner's
  screenshot against the model: predicted gaps 54.0 px and 81.2 px against 54 px and 81 px measured,
  cells summing to 1076 px against a 1075 px strip.
- **A segment could quote `availability 100%` over a range it never materialized.** This is a defect
  in the OLD numbers, not in the new drawing, and it was invisible for as long as every segment strip
  was normalised to full width. It surfaced only because lanes on a real time axis put 27 days of
  hatch beside the 100%. `coverage = 100%` says every observation that EXISTS was decidable, which a
  hole makes worthless as a warrant; §11.2 makes the two axes independent with both required.
- **The `/sla` card carries two P0 tenant-context defects nobody reported.**
  `resetMaintenanceState` resets seven pieces of editor state and its own comment claims to cover
  every one, but not the card's three — so a value typed for project A was still in the box under
  project B, where Save wrote it as a perfectly legitimate write the store cannot refuse. And
  neither of the card's writers took a load generation, while every neighbour in that file does and
  one says why ("the project moved under this response").

## 3. The line the owner asked for, and why it cannot be earned

He chose to bring the contiguity fact into this iteration rather than take it as a follow-up — on a
cost estimate of this session's that was **wrong** ("no migration needed"). The design was rejected
on three readings of the tree, two of them this session's own misreadings:

- `internal/prober/prober.go` runs `Retries + 1` attempts, each under its own
  `context.WithTimeout(m.Timeout())`, so a run may occupy `(retries+1) × timeout`.
- `result.allowed_skew` is only step 4b's clock test in `internal/store/monitors.go` — a bound on how
  far *before* its job's issue an observation may claim to be. It bounds neither queue delay nor
  delivery, and using it in a spacing allowance was not a stretch of its meaning but the wrong
  quantity entirely.
- `job_issued_at` is **not a column of `heartbeats`**. It rides the wire for correlation and is gone
  after ingest, so no row anywhere records that a run was ever *expected*.

Two received heartbeats therefore bound **observed spacing** and nothing more. The corrected cost
went back to the owner and he re-decided for the honest panel. **FR-032**
(`docs/specs/func-expected-run-ledger.md`, `NOT DESIGNED`) is what would earn a stroke back.

## 5. Six reviewer P1s, and what each cost

Recorded because the drawing gates an implementation and three of these were defects *in the
drawing*.

1. **The `NFR-025` contradiction** — the specification asserted an instance-wide invariant while §9
   deliberately excluded five call sites. Resolved by scoping the SHIPPED invariant (`NFR-025a`) and
   keeping the follow-up (`NFR-025b`) with its own identifier and open status, not by weakening it.
2. **The fixture disagreed with its own KPI** — 2770 stored buckets against a drawn `2873 of 43200`.
   The fixture now totals 2873 and `sealed_through` is day-aligned so every figure is hand-checkable.
3. **An axis identity label overwrote a fact** at 1440px. Identity labels live on their own line now.
4. **A `×3` that could not be true of a four-minute cluster** — only two of three lane starts are
   transitions inside the window; the third is the window's own left edge, four weeks away.
5. **Figures borrowed from a screenshot instead of derived** — the mock displaying a number nothing
   computed, which is this iteration's own subject one level up. Every figure on two of the three
   surfaces is now derived from one fixture through the spec's formulas, and the mock's script is
   executed under a DOM stub while preparing it.
6. **`availability` quoted over unmaterialized time** (§2 above), which is the finding that justified
   the geometry change on its own.

Two of this session's **own forbidden inferences** are recorded the same way: a pixel threshold that
was a density-dependent selector in a legibility costume (16.5 px/min against a 12 px rule marks all
59 intervals), and prose reading "6-minute hole", a count of checks it could not know were due — now
"widest interval between two recorded checks: 7 minutes".

## 6. Defects the tests caught before anything was visible

- **The floor was paid in stack order**, which takes from provisional good first and zeroes it —
  making unsealed time vanish to pay for a floor. Payment order is explicit now, sealed good first.
- **The stack divided by the CLIPPED extent**, so a window starting mid-step overflowed its first
  cell by the ratio of the step to the visible part. The denominator is the larger of extent and
  durations, which is right in both directions.
- **A stray `<rect>` inside the hatch `<pattern>`** was the first rect in document order, so every
  `find("rect")` in a test reached it instead of a cell.
- **Save disabled for an invalid draft left the operator unable to learn why** — my own regression,
  caught by an existing test. The refusal is met at the field, live.

## 7. One boundary the reviewer moved during implementation

The per-segment withholding explanation was going to carry the payload's canonical codes
(`window_precedes_materialization_era` / `storage_gap`) derived in the component. Flagged when
implementation started rather than discovered in review, and ruled out at party [176]: a client may
derive presentation from the series it holds, but it may not manufacture a wire verdict whose
canonical meaning belongs server-side. It is display grammar with no code identity now, under an
explicit fence — it never leaves the component, never enters metrics, audit or the API, and never
drives reliability state.
