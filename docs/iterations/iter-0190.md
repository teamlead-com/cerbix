  context on helper return, allowing leader step-down to clear readiness before caller assertions.

  and joins in `t.Cleanup`, and the regression waits for both the rendered not-ready gauge and the
  specific lagging reason.
- The scheduler regression passes 1000 ordinary repetitions, 200 repetitions under `-race`, and two
  consecutive full `make race` runs.
