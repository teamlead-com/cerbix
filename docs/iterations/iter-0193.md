## Final whole-branch review residual and technical closure

The final whole-branch review originally left one **Important** Incident Detail residual: cold `init()`
could increment generation before a pending marker existed, the view could passively reopen initialization
after terminal failure, and the incident response was published without proving project ownership. The
original tests used a side-effect-free workspace mock and one late-response deferred was never awaited.

The follow-up work preserved that history and closed the mechanism:

- a named initial-read sentinel is published synchronously with generation before `/organizations`;
- concurrent `init()` callers await one signal, receive the same failure and do not open a passive retry;
- Incident Detail captures generation/project after `ws.init()`, checks `incident.project_id` before
  publishing incident/updates/postmortem, and starts subject/preceded reads only after that fence;
- a real-Pinia integration spec covers cold success, cold failure and project mismatch;
- the late incident test waits real deferred requests, and the older workspace-generation test opens both
  old/new requests, resolves the old one last and kills a controlled missing-fence mutation.

A fresh independent scoped review marked all seven blocker criteria **ADDRESSED**, found no new
Critical or Important findings, and returned **APPROVED**. The owner completed visual/keyboard sign-off on
2026-10-01; the post-verdict docs-only review is **APPROVED**, and iter-0193 is closed with the documented
skips below.
